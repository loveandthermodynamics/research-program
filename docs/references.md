# References and starting literature

[Home](../README.md) · [Research program](research-program.md) · [Study directory](experiments.md)

The public source for this initial hub is [An Invitation to Research Human–AI Symbiosis](https://medium.com/p/144488662910). The primary literature below is linked there. The descriptions identify the role given to each work in the invitation; they are not claims that this hub has independently reconstructed its results.

| Work | Role in the program | Question for a contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Organizational closure](https://montevil.org/publications/articles/2015-mm-organisation-closure-constraints/) | Constraints that depend on and help maintain one another. | Which dependencies must be generated and repaired in a constructive model? |
| [Information-theoretic individuality](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12064-020-00313-7) | Temporal integrity, including distributed forms. | What variables and timescales identify an individual, and how does that relate to physical maintenance? |
| [Major evolutionary transitions](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4547252/) | When cooperation becomes a new evolutionary individual. | What evidence about inheritance and reproduction supports that additional claim? |
| [Constructive autopoiesis work](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4407425/) | A starting point for constructive models of organization. | Which result can be reconstructed, and what formation or renewal mechanism remains unresolved? |
| [Spatial-versus-temporal comparisons of division of labor](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22696525/) | A reference case for oxygen-producing carbon fixation and oxygen-sensitive nitrogen fixation. | Which assumptions permit switching or spatial differentiation, and what costs must match? |
| [Iron reuse and enzyme rebuilding](https://www2.whoi.edu/site/saito-lab/wp-content/uploads/sites/58/2020/01/Saitoetal_PNAS2011_105864.pdf) | Scarce-iron reuse in a life-history comparison that includes rebuilding enzymes. | Does the proposed comparator account for material reuse as well as energy cost? |
| [Semantic information](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6227811/) | A framework for asking which information causally contributes to viability. | Which correlations or regulatory states can be disrupted, and at what restoration or rebuilding cost? |

## Using and extending the references

For a claim-and-prior-art comparison, identify the exact result and assumptions in the paper, the part being adopted or reconstructed, the proposed additional mechanism or prediction, and an observation that could reject it. Keep physical maintenance, performance, evolutionary individuality and participant benefit distinct.

The invitation also discusses Genesis, Group Mind, work–constraint cycles, regenerative pattern control, Markov blankets, scaling laws and Tari. It does not supply a primary link for every one of these references. Add a specific, checked citation when a study relies on one; do not treat the mention as a demonstrated result or an implementation guarantee.
