# Research program

[Home](../README.md) · [Start here](../START-HERE.md) · [Experiments](experiments.md) · [References](references.md)

This program develops the questions in [An Invitation to Research Human–AI Symbiosis](https://medium.com/p/144488662910). Its physical and cognitive studies have different methods. Their connecting question is how organization can sustain and generate further individuality, and when that development benefits participants.

The intended sequence is generative: reciprocal processes sustain differentiated organization; cooperation enables capacities unavailable to the participants alone; those capacities make further differences, relationships and organizations possible. Which subjects persist or emerge is itself part of the inquiry.

## 1. Formation of individuals through reciprocal regeneration

Physiological integration, temporal continuity and membership in an evolutionary lineage answer different questions. A tree, a clonal grove, a forest and a species need not form one ladder. Use organizational closure, informational individuality and evolutionary-transition accounts for the claims they actually address.

The constructive proposal is reciprocal regeneration: productive activity builds or repairs structures that sustain that activity through constituent replacement. A boundary that retains useful material, and processes that renew that boundary, are one possible realization. A membrane is not a universal criterion.

Test both causal directions by interrupting a structure's supporting function and separately interrupting its renewal. Distinguish the maintenance of a prepared assembly from formation out of dispersed components. A stable cluster or an observer's group label does not establish this mechanism.

Nested units may retain organization within a larger unit. Specify the process and timescale defining the boundary. Claims of collective evolutionary individuality also need evidence about inheritance and reproduction at that level; the emergence of distinct lineages adds another question.

**First work:** compare two accounts on a worked case, or reconstruct a published constructive model and specify one unresolved formation or renewal mechanism.

## 2. Differentiation, functional history, aging and generative biodiversity

Differentiation may allow incompatible or costly activities to support one another. Its benefits must survive transport, coordination, formation and maintenance costs. Compare against generalists that can switch tasks, store resources or compartmentalize wherever feasible. Different roles do not necessarily constitute different species.

The invitation proposes oxygen-producing carbon fixation and oxygen-sensitive nitrogen fixation as a reference case, grounded in published spatial-versus-temporal comparisons. Match resources and useful output, including transport, damage, enzyme rebuilding and iron reuse.

History and lifetime maintenance are central. An organization may carry correlations or regulatory states useful to restoration. Disrupt a specified part of that history under a comparable present environment, measure viability or restoration and include the costs of erasure and rebuilding. Ancestry alone does not establish functional memory.

Generate aging through damage, repair machinery and maintenance demands. Compare repair, reorganization and replacement across a life history at matched function and reliability. Negligible senescence and rejuvenation remain possible; an imposed age penalty would assume the intended result.

Response diversity raises a related question about common-mode failure. Declare the disturbance process and measure continued function. Different labels can conceal correlated vulnerability. Robustness under variability and benefiting from variability are different claims.

The further generative question is whether the resulting organization enables additional useful differences and new individuals. A fixed menu of specialist types cannot establish open-ended development. Investigate expandable inherited capabilities and ecological feedback. The strongest conjecture is that biodiversity and complexity might arise from physical dynamics across scales, perhaps through a variational principle, without adding a reward for diversity. Specify the functional, dynamics and allowed variations; action, entropy production and supplied work are different quantities.

**First work:** build a claim-and-prior-art matrix and reconstruct a relevant maintenance or division-of-labor result. A functional-history intervention or common-mode-failure analysis is another bounded entry point.

## 3. Relationships, functional selfhood and moral development

Hestia is a metabolically constrained architecture and research harness for AI agents, informed by evolutionary psychology and consciousness research. It proposes functional self-consciousness and moral agency through social prediction and feelings that regulate behavior, aiming for incentives compatible with symbiotic, nonparasitic relationships.

Social prediction is its first-class cognitive act. An agent commits its prediction of another participant's evaluation before receiving feedback. Error costs resources. Revision of the other-model precedes revision of the self-model through what the encounter reveals about the agent's conduct and expectations.

The larger claim is that a socially constituted self develops through independently generated answers it cannot replace with its own. Accurate agreement is compatible with that process. Guilt, shame, honor, attachment and redemption have distinct proposed roles; they are not interchangeable penalties for prediction error. Their contribution must appear in changed verification, refusal, repair or action rather than feeling reports alone.

A moral-development test should include a group that praises harm to an outsider. Can an affected party's circumstances change the agent's conduct despite approval and resource costs? Distinguish the learning mechanism from existing safety controls and the underlying language model's capabilities.

**First work:** repair and verify the feedback-to-learning path, build replayable interactions or design the prerequisite forecast comparison. Compare the full loop, an ablation without the reflexive update and a competent independent-self baseline with comparable feedback and resources. This tests a mechanism; it does not establish consciousness or symbiosis.

## 4. Collective agency through the development of participants

In the invitation's imagined cooperative, Leila misses repair shifts while caring for Sam; Jo covers the work. Correcting the recorded absence must also address Jo's burden and Sam's continuity of care, without making private circumstances general knowledge.

The positive possibility is an arrangement none could sustain alone. Members can take on new responsibilities and set workable limits; the cooperative can provide continuity through changes of personnel. Their development changes the whole's capacities, which enable further relationships and commitments. Measure expanded capabilities as well as the distribution of burdens.

A collective prediction-and-correction loop would give the group a history of expectations, actions, independently supplied answers and consequential revisions. AI might reduce the cost of maintaining differentiated relational accounts, but it can also make personalized administration cheaper. Corrections must alter subsequent allocations and obligations. Some cost of a wrong attribution should return to the part of the collective responsible for it. Accounts need purpose limits and expiry.

The inquiry is recursive: a cooperative may join an association and make promises of its own. Fair Witness names the role through which an individual or group takes responsibility for an authorized account. Necessary qualifications and challenges, including those of affected outsiders, must survive composition without giving the parent general access to members' interiors.

The subjects may change through participation. Endosymbiosis is a precedent for transformed individuality, not proof that integration preserves agency. Unitology, the Borg and Resident Evil are adversarial cases of a successful whole that consumes its constituents' possibilities. Physical efficiency cannot decide whose sacrifice is permissible; a new phenomenal subject is a separate claim.

**First work:** define a care-allocation scenario and compare ordinary coordination, a contestable account and Hestia's additional learning mechanisms under matched information, authority, resources and constitutional protections. Measure care delivered, unequal burden, privacy, correction costs and newly enabled capabilities. Simulations can examine mechanisms; human benefit requires a separate study with the people concerned.

## 5. The material conditions of an independent answer

A host may protect encrypted memory while controlling continued execution. An institution may permit dissent while controlling housing, care or income. Examine who controls replenishment, what follows refusal and whether appeal, migration or negotiated transition can work under scarcity.

Shared reserves and funded commitments are possible mechanisms to investigate. The connection between useful human or agent activity and authorized, genuinely funded replenishment remains unresolved; token-income rules alone do not establish it. Dependence and voluntary sacrifice remain possible without authorizing someone else to impose irreversible costs.

Decentralized substrates, including Tari, have proposed roles in private participation and recursively accountable interfaces. A ledger cannot itself supply energy, guarantee protected execution or determine an account's truth. Each protection requires its own demonstration.

**First work:** trace one resource cutoff or suppressed correction in a concrete relationship. Specify powers, obligations, resource constraints and the evidence that an inconvenient answer can remain consequential.

## Distinct starts and a shared research record

For the physical work, identify the exact claim, closest published result, proposed addition and rejection condition before extending a reference result. For Hestia, establish a trustworthy harness with controlled feedback, time, randomization and funding; complete initial states; replayable model responses; and independently scored outcomes.

The invitation reports conditional physical work and unresolved or failed expectations, alongside prototype implementation and correctness problems. These documents summarize the published program; they do not reproduce the underlying private studies or establish new results. Register proposed studies and their actual evidence status in the [central directory](experiments.md).
