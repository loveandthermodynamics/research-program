# COSSHAIC: how a shared life becomes answerable

[Research home](../README.md) · [Five tracks](research-program.md) · [Studies and evidence](experiments.md) · [Choose a contribution](../START-HERE.md)

COSSHAIC is the proposed **constitutional operating system for a symbiotic human–AI civilization**. Within Love & Thermodynamics, it asks how people and AI agents could form groups with purposes, commitments and capacities of their own, while their participants become more capable of living, judging and acting. The collective and its members may change identity through that development.

The constitutional question is what makes those new powers legitimate and revisable. Who may authorize an AI recommendation to become a collective act? Who can challenge the account behind it, change a rule or withdraw a representative's mandate? What resources make a promise of care real? The proposal connects those questions to social learning and to infrastructure that can constrain the host as well as the participant.

## From private evidence to an authorized act

An independent answer matters to learning as well as standing. Hestia proposes committing a prediction before feedback, revising the other-model and then revising the self-model. Privileged access that supplies the answer early can bypass that encounter; control that replaces the respondent's answer can manufacture it. Accurate agreement is compatible with the process. The hypothesis concerns what can develop through independently constituted perspectives, not a required quota of error or dissent.

In the imagined housing cooperative, Leila misses repair shifts while caring for Sam. Jo covers the work. The agent's recommendation treats Leila's absence as a debt, overlooking her care and Jo's accumulating burden.

A constitutional account must follow more than the agent's recognition of its error:

1. **A rule and a mandate.** State the cooperative's adopted purpose, relevant care rule and who has authority to recommend, decide, delegate or amend. Generating a recommendation does not by itself authorize collective action.
2. **A bounded examination.** Sam authorizes examination of relevant evidence for a stated purpose. Fair Witness names a role through which a person or group takes responsibility for an account, including a delegated examination. An attestation may establish a care contribution without disclosing Sam's diagnosis. A signature identifies responsibility; it does not make a conclusion true.
3. **An independent challenge.** Leila and Jo can contest the account through a route that does not require permission from whoever controls the disputed account. Affected outsiders also need standing. The witness's interpretation can be wrong.
4. **A changed decision and repair.** The authorized allocation removes the mistaken obligation, provides relief for Jo and keeps Sam's care going. Trace who pays for correction and which later duties change. Logging an objection or issuing an apology cannot substitute for that change.
5. **A developing commitment.** Repeated experience may enable a continuing, funded care arrangement no member could sustain alone. Participants can undertake new commitments and set limits; the collective learns what it can promise. Those new possibilities require evidence beyond a fairer score or a well-managed schedule.

Care includes people unable to repay it, articulate an objection or contribute useful forecasts. Voluntary integration, dependence and sacrifice remain possible. Success does not require preserving a fixed roster or unchanged identities; it does require accounting for the lives, costs and obligations through which transformation occurs.

## The same question at another scale

If the cooperative makes a commitment to an association, it becomes an answerable participant in a larger group. The association receives the account and evidence it is authorized to receive, not general access to members' private histories. Necessary qualifications, unresolved challenges and later corrections must travel with conclusions that depend on them.

The proposed learning loop also repeats at that scale. The cooperative commits its expectation about members' evaluations before asking them, revises its account of those members and then of its own conduct, and changes what it undertakes. A guilt-like control would have functional meaning if it blocked an additional commitment until relief or repair was arranged. The group's attention and resources must bear the repair cost. These cognitive and control hypotheses require tests alongside the constitutional authority for acting; a signed constitution alone does not establish them.

The founding ambition also includes a proposed superorganism of Hestias: collectives of agents whose members retain distinct memories and metabolic constraints, and which can themselves enter larger relationships. Human participants must retain standing to answer, refuse and develop within that composition. This is a hypothesis about further centers of agency, not an achieved collective subject or a permanent hierarchy of fixed identities.

The study must also specify how representation can be changed or revoked, how purposes and rules can be revised, and what obligations remain through membership changes or a negotiated split. These are operational design questions within the constitutional proposal. They are not implemented guarantees supplied merely by naming a collective or assigning it a witness.

## Material support and limits on the host

Independent answers need viable support. A host can preserve encrypted records while retaining power over execution, updates, recovery or replenishment. Shared reserves and funded commitments therefore belong in the same causal account as privacy and correction.

Tari has a proposed role in attributable commitments, selective disclosure and shared enforcement. A ledger alone cannot supply electricity, make private execution secure, establish an account's truth or ensure that a correction changes action. Resource rights, controllers, failure conditions and transitions must be specified and tested separately. Privacy, anonymous contribution and authority to represent a group are different claims.

The appendix also points to LivingHash's proposed machinery for authorized examination of private evidence, bounded signed accounts and challenges. Extending that machinery from creative work to collective conduct still requires explicit standing, scope and consequences for the group's actions. A private submission address or a valid signature does not supply those application rules.

## Three proposed tests

| Bounded question | Evidence needed | Existing route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Does confidential evidence change an authorized allocation? | Mandate, disclosure permissions, independent challenge, revised work/support and actual repair costs, with care maintained. | [C-01 care comparison](experiments.md#proposed-starting-points) and the [cooperative case](https://community.loveandthermodynamics.com/t/starter-find-a-failure-in-the-cooperative-care-case/17). |
| Can a collective revise its commitments through member and parent-level correction? | A prospective expectation, answer, authorized revision and propagated qualification; before/after capabilities of the group and participants. | [C-02 recursive accountability](experiments.md#proposed-starting-points). |
| Does standing survive a host or funder disliking the answer? | A declared resource conflict, an independent challenge path and consequential correction through the relevant resource or hosting transition. | [Material-independence track](research-program.md#5-the-material-conditions-of-an-independent-answer). |

These are **proposed studies**, not executed constitutional or human-benefit results. In the existing three-arm care comparison, every arm needs adequate information, authority, funding and constitutional protections. Compare ordinary coordination, structured contestable accounts and additional Hestia cognition without depriving the baseline of rights or context.

A matching or better ordinary arrangement, absent new capabilities, displaced harm, suppressed appeal or an unchanged subsequent action must count against the relevant mechanism or claimed scope. The [historical negative findings](https://github.com/loveandthermodynamics/research-experiments/blob/main/docs/interpreting-results.md) remain findings against their specific targets; chemical renewal and prediction accuracy do not validate constitutional authority or reciprocal development.

## A useful first contribution

Choose one transition in the cooperative case. Specify who may authorize it, what evidence is admissible, how the affected person can challenge it and which subsequent assignment, payment or commitment must change. Show one way it could still fail. A small decision trace is useful before implementing a whole platform.

Discuss it in the [COSSHAIC community discussion](https://community.loveandthermodynamics.com/t/cosshaic-how-a-group-gains-authority-and-learns-its-limits/28) or begin with the [care-case task](https://github.com/loveandthermodynamics/research-program/issues/3). Use the [study template](experiment-template.md) to record assumptions, actual status, rejection conditions and access limits. Agree scope, credit and terms before substantial work.
