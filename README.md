# Love & Thermodynamics Research

How does living organization create possibilities for further life, individuality and cooperation? How could a collective develop capacities of its own through the continuing development of its participants?

This research hub connects physical regeneration and biodiversity with Hestia's investigation of functional selfhood, moral development and human–AI groups. The participants, their relationships and the scale of individuality may all change through cooperation. The aim is to explain that development and test when participation is beneficial.

Read the founding invitation: [An Invitation to Research Human–AI Symbiosis](https://medium.com/p/144488662910).

## Five research tracks

| Track | Central question | An entry point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Formation of individuals** | Can reciprocal regeneration produce an organization whose productive activity renews the structures that sustain it through constituent replacement? | Compare accounts of individuality on one case, or reconstruct a constructive model. |
| **2. Differentiation, history and biodiversity** | When does differentiation repay its costs over a life history, and how could organization generate further useful differences and individuals? | Reconstruct a comparison, design a functional-history intervention, or examine a shared vulnerability. |
| **3. Relational selfhood and moral development** | Can social prediction and distinct behavior-regulating feelings support functional selfhood and symbiotic conduct? | Trace one feedback correction, specify a competing predictor, or design a moral-development comparison. |
| **4. Collective agency and further individuation** | Can a group acquire commitments and capacities of its own while expanding its members' capabilities and remaining answerable to them? | Develop the care-allocation case and its recursive extension to an association. |
| **5. Material independence** | What makes an independently generated answer consequential when another party controls continued support or execution? | Trace one resource dependency, refusal, appeal or transition. |

The physical ambition includes investigating whether dynamics could generate biodiversity and complexity across scales without an added reward for diversity. It remains a conjecture. A collective's survival, efficiency or intelligence does not by itself establish participant flourishing. These questions require different methods; a calibration result cannot establish the whole program.

## Where the work stands

The invitation reports a philosophical corpus, a protocol, prototype code, conditional mathematical work and saved negative results. Some maintenance and renewal claims have support within that work; formation and specialist-advantage expectations have failed or remain unresolved. A universal variational derivation and a novel contribution to the field have not been established.

The immediate physical task is a claim-and-prior-art matrix and reconstruction of a relevant published result. The immediate software task is to make Hestia a trustworthy experimental harness before interpreting its outputs scientifically. [The directory](docs/experiments.md) keeps these starting points separate.

This hub contains coordination and research documents. Hestia implementation access and reuse terms are agreed separately; its code is currently private.

## Discussion and research records

The Discourse research forum is being configured; its link will appear here. Until then, start a question through the [issue chooser](https://github.com/Lifefable/research-program/issues/new/choose). The forum will be the home for developing questions and interpreting work. Use this hub's issues for a defined contribution and pull requests for proposed document changes. Link a discussion to its study record so the question, method, artifacts and interpretation remain connected.

Before substantial work, agree scope, credit, access and reuse terms, including any compensation. A reference or a well-specified question is enough to begin.
