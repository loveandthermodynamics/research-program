# Contributing

[Home](README.md) · [Starter contributions](START-HERE.md) · [Experiment template](docs/experiment-template.md)

The program welcomes strong alternatives, counterexamples, replications and negative outcomes alongside positive findings. A result that narrows a claim is useful when its method and limits remain inspectable.

## From a question to a contribution

1. Read the relevant [research track](docs/research-program.md), check the [study directory](docs/experiments.md), and consult the [historical catalogue](https://github.com/loveandthermodynamics/research-experiments/blob/main/CATALOGUE.md) for related work and its limits.
2. Propose a bounded question in a hub issue or the [research forum](README.md#discussion-and-research-records). Link the paper, case or study you would start from.
3. Agree the deliverable, scope, contributor roles, access, credit and reuse terms, including any compensation, before substantial work. Contributors should be able to question the framing and priorities as well as supply answers.
4. For an experiment, record the design and rejection conditions before outcome comparisons. Use the [template](docs/experiment-template.md), with fields marked not applicable where appropriate.
5. Submit an inspectable record and link it from the directory. Separate the proposed method, completed work, findings and interpretation. Record review and replication as distinct events.

## Documents and discussions

Use an issue for a defined task, a forum topic for discussion and a pull request for a proposed document change. Cross-link them when they concern the same claim. A review should identify the passage or artifact at issue and explain what correction it requires.

For a literature contribution, provide the exact reference, the result being used, its assumptions and what it leaves unresolved. Existing explanations should become foundations. A new combination of familiar ideas is a proposal to evaluate, not an automatic claim of novelty.

Standalone exploratory scripts and curated outputs can be inspected in the separate [research-experiments archive](https://github.com/loveandthermodynamics/research-experiments). Read its [reproduction guide](https://github.com/loveandthermodynamics/research-experiments/blob/main/REPRODUCING.md) and [retained-rights notice](https://github.com/loveandthermodynamics/research-experiments/blob/main/NOTICE.md). Existing source notices remain in place, no new license is added, and unresolved reuse terms require agreement.

For work depending on the private Hestia runtime, first arrange implementation access and agree which artifacts can be shared. Public reports should contain only material authorized for public release; describe access-dependent evidence accurately rather than implying that the exploratory scripts include the full runtime or bulk raw histories.

## Evidence and interpretation

For human–AI or collective proposals, identify which [COSSHAIC constitutional mechanism](docs/cosshaic.md) is involved, if any. Follow authority, scoped evidence, challenge and repair into the actual act or resource commitment. Distinguish that chain from agent calibration, a signed record or an assertion of collective identity. State which constitutional protections are held adequate and equal across comparators. These fields are not mandatory for an unrelated chemical calculation; mark them not applicable with a reason.

Keep these distinctions explicit:

- Formation from dispersed components and maintenance of a prepared assembly.
- Differentiation, taxonomic biodiversity, physical integrity and functional performance.
- Predictive calibration, functional selfhood, moral development and subjective experience.
- Collective fitness or engagement and the welfare and development of participants.
- A proposed experiment, an executed run, an independent replay and external review.

Name what was measured and preserve the record supporting it. Match relevant information, resources, authority, useful function and reliability across comparisons. State the allowed alternative strategies; do not restrict a comparator merely to obtain the preferred outcome.

## People, private circumstances and consent

Use the imagined cooperative case or other clearly labeled simulations when developing an initial scenario. Learning about human benefit requires a separate study with the people concerned. Define its scope, permissions, data handling and review before collecting participant material.

Keep private circumstances out of public issues and traces. A correction can be consequential without making its underlying personal information general institutional knowledge. Identify which evidence is public, shared by agreement or unavailable, and how that affects what others can verify.

## Completing a contribution

A handoff should include the agreed deliverable, source and artifact links, its status, results and limits, unresolved questions, and the next action and owner if agreed. Update the directory without presenting a proposed or unfinished study as completed. Preserve differences of interpretation and link later corrections to the claims and decisions that depended on them.
