# Start here

[Home](README.md) · [Program](docs/research-program.md) · [Study directory](docs/experiments.md)

Begin with one question and a small deliverable. You do not need to adopt the whole program or have access to Hestia code to contribute.

For the constitutional work, begin with [COSSHAIC's role and proposed tests](docs/cosshaic.md). A useful first artifact is a short decision trace: who authorizes a group commitment, what bounded evidence can challenge it, and which subsequent assignment, funded support or promise must change. The collective's and participants' identities and purposes may develop; record what they can newly undertake, rather than assuming a fixed membership roster.

To build on existing work, start with the [historical experiment catalogue](https://github.com/loveandthermodynamics/research-experiments/blob/main/CATALOGUE.md) or the [five topic digests](docs/experiments.md#result-directory). They distinguish saved results, failed comparisons, conditional proofs and proposed studies. The [reproduction guide](https://github.com/loveandthermodynamics/research-experiments/blob/main/REPRODUCING.md) explains which checks use standalone public scripts and which require private Hestia access or omitted raw histories.

## Choose an entry point

| Starting contribution | Useful background | First deliverable | What makes it useful |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Trace a constitutional decision** | Cooperatives, institutional design, law, governance, software state transitions | One care-case trace naming the mandate, evidence permissions, authorized decision, independent challenge and changed work/support. | Show who can change or revoke authority, who pays for repair, and one failure that still remains; keep it a proposed design until executed. |
| **Compare two accounts of individuality** | Philosophy of biology, theoretical biology, information theory | One worked case identifying the accounts, their criteria and the observations each needs. | Explain whether they disagree or answer different questions; name an observation that could resolve a genuine disagreement. |
| **Reconstruct a physical reference result** | Ecology, mathematical biology, nonequilibrium physics | A short derivation or reproducible reconstruction of one result in the [linked literature](docs/references.md), with its assumptions and comparator. | Separate what is already established from one proposed extension; include a condition that would reject the extension. |
| **Design a history or aging intervention** | Information theory, regeneration, life-history research | A specified regulatory state or correlation to disrupt, an outcome and a comparison under the same present environment. | Show how the intervention distinguishes function-bearing history from ancestry, and account for erasure, repair and rebuilding costs. |
| **Trace one feedback correction** | Software engineering, experimental agents | A regression and trace showing whether a correction reaches the state used in subsequent decisions. | Name the expected state transition and independently observable effect. Arrange implementation access before taking on this task. |
| **Challenge the reflexive-learning baseline** | Cognition, machine learning, evaluation | A comparison design stating the alternative predictor's information, resource budget, forecasts and scoring rule. | Give it comparable feedback and resources; distinguish useful forecasts from a stored average of previous errors. |
| **Pressure-test the care arrangement** | Care, cooperatives, institutions, collective intelligence | One failure remaining after a seemingly successful correction, and a proposed allocation or support change. | Include the person receiving care, the person providing it and the person covering other work. Define both relief and a new capacity the arrangement could enable. |
| **Trace a material dependency** | Security, distributed systems, institutional design | One relationship showing who controls a resource, what follows refusal and how an appeal or transition could work. | State the adversary or institutional power, resource constraints and the evidence that an inconvenient answer remains consequential. |

These are starter specifications, not assignments or claims that experiments have already run. The [directory](docs/experiments.md) gives them places in the program.

## Propose the work

Open a hub issue with a descriptive title, or start a discussion through the [forum link on the home page](README.md#discussion-and-research-records). Include:

1. The track and precise question.
2. The starting contribution you propose, or a reference that changes it.
3. The smallest deliverable you can specify and the materials you would need.
4. Whether you seek a literature discussion, a collaborator, review or implementation access.

A short proposal is enough. There is no need to commit to a larger study before its scope is agreed.

## Before substantial work

These are volunteer contributions to an [unfunded program](README.md#funding-and-control). Use [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md) to agree scope, credit, access and reuse terms. Public exploratory scripts retain their original rights notices without a new license. Full Hestia runtime access is agreed separately; a public discussion does not confer access to private implementation or research records.

For a proposed experiment, copy the [experiment template](docs/experiment-template.md). Record its initial status as proposed, link the discussion and register it in the directory when its scope and owner are agreed.
